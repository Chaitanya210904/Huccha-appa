HOW TO RUN:
-----------
1. On Windows:
   Double-click "Start.bat"

2. On Mac:
   Double-click "start.command"

3. On Linux:
   Run "./start.sh" in a terminal

The application runs entirely on your local machine at:
http://127.0.0.1:3456

INTERNET ACCESS:
----------------
No internet connection is ever required. All astronomical calculations,
Panchang timings, and Shraddha evaluations operate 100% offline.

DATA SAFETY:
------------
All client records, ancestors, bookings, and receipts are stored safely
inside the "data/" directory. Rolling automated backups are kept inside
the "backups/" folder.

You can download a full backup ZIP at any time from the "Backup & Restore"
page to keep on a USB pen drive.
